How to move
Phone: drag one finger to move · pinch to zoom · twist two fingers to turn · drag two fingers up/down to tilt · double-tap to zoom in · two-finger tap to zoom out · compass = north, tap again = straight down · 🚶 then tap a spot for street level (joystick walks, drag looks).
Mouse: left-drag moves · right-drag or Ctrl/Shift+drag turns and tilts · wheel zooms to the cursor · double-click flies there · WASD/arrows move, Q/E turn, R/F tilt. Street level: WASD + drag to look, Shift runs.
